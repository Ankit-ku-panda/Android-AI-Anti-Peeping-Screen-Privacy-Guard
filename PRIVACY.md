# Privacy Policy

PrivacyGuard AI processes front-camera frames only on the device for face-count detection.

- No photo or video is saved.
- No face template or identity profile is created.
- No analytics or advertising SDK is included.
- The application does not request Android's internet permission.
- Camera frames are closed immediately after the bundled detector finishes each analysis.

The app reports only a temporary face count to its in-memory risk engine. That value is not written to storage.

The front camera remains active only while the user-started foreground protection service is running. Android displays a persistent notification during that time. The user can stop protection at any time from the app, notification, or shield.
